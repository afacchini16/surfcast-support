SurfCast Support

Surf forecasts for over 400 breaks, plus Apple Watch session tracking.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email surfcastsupport@gmail.com and include your iPhone and Apple Watch models. I usually reply within a few days.

Recording a session

  • Open SurfCast on your Apple Watch and tap Start Surf before you paddle out. Tap End when you're done.
  • Turn on Water Lock yourself (swipe up for Control Center) so water doesn't press buttons.
  • Sessions shorter than 10 minutes aren't saved.
  • The session appears on your iPhone's Sessions tab the next time both devices are nearby with SurfCast open.

Common questions

A session didn't show up on my iPhone. Open SurfCast on both the watch and the iPhone, keep them close together, and wait a minute.

Some waves weren't counted, or something that wasn't a wave was. Waves are detected from sudden jumps in GPS speed. Short rides in small surf can be missed. Enter the waves you counted on the watch's summary to compare.

My session time is shorter than I expected. If you walked to or from the water before tapping Start or End, SurfCast leaves out the time on land. Tap Undo on the session's Time on Land card to see the whole recording.

Spots are sorted from the wrong place. Allow location in Settings → SurfCast → Location. Without it, spots are sorted from Del Mar, California.

How do I see the welcome tour again? On the Nearby tab, tap the flag at the top left, then Welcome Tour.

SurfCast Pro

  • Forecasts are free. Your first 3 watch sessions are free; Pro unlocks unlimited sessions (monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase).
  • New iPhone or reinstalled the app? On the Sessions tab, tap the crown, then Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel a subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Safety

Forecasts and ratings are estimates from weather models and can be wrong. Always check conditions in person and surf within your ability.
